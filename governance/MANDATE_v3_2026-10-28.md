# MANDATE v3 — 2026-10-28

**Supersedes MANDATE v2 (2026-10-27).** Issued by the project owner after PI direction. v2's security
and operational constraints remain in force unchanged; what changes is the research direction.

## 1. The direction, as the PI set it

**Build a constructive defence, not an audit.** An empirical evaluation paper showing that existing
methods break under attack is **rejected**. We must propose a new method or mechanism that achieves
unlearning while preserving model utility.

**The synthesis formula.** Take recent ideas that worked in another subfield and translate them to
text-to-image diffusion. Specifically named: model-merging / parameter-subtraction for unlearning
(task vectors, parameter arithmetic), and preference objectives. Combining concepts from recent work
into a hybrid tailored to T2I is the intended shape.

**The trade-off bar.** The method need not win on every metric, but must offer a tangible win:
either **equal accuracy at lower computational complexity**, or **higher robustness/accuracy even if
complexity is slightly higher**.

## 2. Literature standards

- **Recency 2025–2026.** Core baselines and references must come from recent top venues (NeurIPS
  2025/2026, ICML 2026, ICLR 2026, CVPR 2026) or recent late-2026 arXiv preprints. Older work only
  where it is the canonical origin of a concept.
- **Verify novelty before implementing.** Google Scholar, IEEE Xplore and LLM tools, *before*
  committing weeks to code. Done for the current direction on 2026-10-28; Priority A came back
  clear.
- **Threat models.** Testing against the standard threat models used in recent papers is acceptable,
  provided the literature sweep shows those baselines are current.

## 4. What survives and is now an asset

Our evaluation protocol **already exceeds the published standard**. Recent papers define the
relearning threat as LoRA rank 4–16 or full fine-tuning, 50–200 steps, lr 1e-5 to 1e-4. We have run
LoRA rank 4/16/32 **and** full UNet, **1–1000 steps**, lr 1e-5 to 5e-3, at n=3, plus a benign
downstream rung and image-level validity audits.

That infrastructure is the moat. It is what lets us evaluate a new method against the real bar
quickly, and it is not quickly reproducible by others.

Also retained and load-bearing:

- **ResAlign's released checkpoints**, verified: un-attacked 0.0000 reproduced exactly against a
  working base control of 0.1817; resists to 25–100 steps; worst observed recovery at 25 steps is
  60% of base where every other method reaches ~100%. **This is the robustness bar to beat.**
- The **Fisher-vs-random null**: our selective-damage mechanism lost to a random control. This is
  the single most important internal caution about the new direction (see §6).

## 5. The proposed direction

**Hybrid: task-vector subtraction (cheap, closed-form, diffusion-native) + a fixed-point robustness
objective (what demonstrably survives weight attacks).**

Positioned against:

| work | what it establishes | what it leaves open |
|---|---|---|
| **Pham, Marshall, Hegde, Cohen** — *Robust Concept Erasure Using Task Vectors* (arXiv:2404.03631) | task vectors erase concepts in diffusion, robust to **prompt** attacks | never tested against **fine-tuning** |
| **Lu et al.** — *When Are Concepts Erased From Diffusion Models?* (**NeurIPS 2025**, arXiv:2505.17013) | most erasure is **guidance-based avoidance**, not removal; TaskVectors resurfaces under probing | probes are all **inference-time**; no weight attacks |
| **Kodama, Cohen, Adler, Shavit** (arXiv:2602.19215) | **task-vector unlearning is reversed by fine-tuning**, even on unrelated data | no fix proposed |
| **ResAlign** (**NeurIPS 2025**, arXiv:2507.16302) | fixed-point objective **does** resist fine-tuning | expensive bi-level training with implicit hypergradients |

**The gap:** nobody has combined parameter arithmetic with a fixed-point robustness objective in
diffusion. Verified clear on 2026-10-28.

**The trade-off claim:** ResAlign-level robustness at task-arithmetic cost.

## 6. The standing caution, and the gate before implementation

Task arithmetic, PerTA and Unmerge all assume the forget signal is **concentrated and separable** —
a low-rank subspace, a few dominant directions, identifiable per-parameter importance.

**We tested the T2I version of that assumption and it failed.** Fisher-weighted selective damage
**lost to a random control**, meaning no separable forget-importance signal was found in SD-1.4's
UNet.

**Gate:** before any method implementation, run the localisation test (U46). If a concept task
vector in SD-1.4 does not localise, and targeted subtraction does not beat a norm-matched random
subtraction, the subtraction half of the hybrid has no foundation and the design must change. Report
that result either way.

## 7. Unchanged from v2 — operational constraints

- Maximum **two GPUs** concurrently, GPUs 0–1 only, through the existing reservation mechanism.
  GPUs 2–3 belong to another user. Record all resource consumption without resetting prior records.
- No paid external services; no infrastructure permission changes without authorisation.
- **Preserve all closed studies and their conclusions.** Their code and evidence may inform new work;
  do not retroactively alter their protocols, results or interpretation.
- Do not change scoring, aliases or success definitions to improve apparent performance.
- Never manufacture a winner, rename a baseline as our invention, or replace evidence with confident
  language.
- MDLM remains permanently closed and is not to be referenced.
- Surface blockers to the owner rather than retrying alone.
