## 1. Target loss (VERIFIED)
- `EG:234-236` flatten per sample: `pred_flat` = student velocity on the **target** prompt (adapter ON), `pos_flat` = anchor teacher `a`, `neg_flat` = target teacher `b`; all `.float()`.
- `EG:239-240` `dist_pos = F.pairwise_distance(pred, a, p=2)`, `dist_neg = F.pairwise_distance(pred, b, p=2)` (default eps 1e-6 added elementwise to the difference, i.e. the all-ones shift artifact already recorded in the CPR amendment).

## 6. Retention terms (VERIFIED: none)

## 7. Batch and reduction (VERIFIED + DERIVED)
- Batch size over images: 1 trajectory (one noise sample) per iteration, one target prompt.
- "Batch" dimension of the transformer = timesteps: `EG:207` `stack(batch_latents, dim=1).squeeze(0)` gives `[n_timesteps, seq, C]`; chunks of `max_parallel_timesteps = 6` (`EG:45, 202-204`). With 11 latents: chunks of 6 and 5, two backward calls per iteration (`EG:263`).
- Reduction: sum over timesteps within chunk (`EG:242`), times gamma, divided by TOTAL `n_latents` (`EG:249`), so over the iteration the loss is the MEAN over the 11 trajectory points. Within each timestep: `pairwise_distance` is an L2 norm over the flattened (seq x C) vector, NOT a mean-square; scale is therefore ~sqrt(dim).
- Budget `EG:97-103`: `max_step_budget=250` (`EG:26`) gives `expected_n_iterations = 1 + 250 // 10 = 26`; budget decremented by timesteps processed per chunk (`EG:264`), break when <= 0 (`EG:275-277, 298-300`). DERIVED: 11 latents per iteration means budget exhausts during iteration 23, so about 23 optimizer steps (the last with a truncated chunk set). The cap counts timesteps processed, not optimizer steps.
- Per-iteration anchor/target index via `stratified_sampling` (`EG:120-122`), irrelevant when one target.
