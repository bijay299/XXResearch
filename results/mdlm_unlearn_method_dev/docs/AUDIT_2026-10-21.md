**Date:** 2026-10-21 · requested mid-session
**Verdict: the direction is right but the contribution is narrower than the headline suggested,
and two things I was about to pursue are now measured as dead ends.**

## 1. What is solid

| result | evidence | strength |
|---|---|---|
| **AFC beats MDU on the forgetting–utility trade-off** | n=3 paired seeds, matched disclosure (0.136 vs 0.133): TV **0.223±0.005 vs 0.352±0.081**, retain **0.576 vs 0.445** | strong |
| **AFC is far more stable** | TV sd 0.005 vs 0.046–0.081 (9–16×); 0/3 seeds breach DEGENERATE ≤0.25 vs 1/3 | strong |
| **The useful half of EMDU's change is the KL direction; FAM is the harmful half** | AFC = anchor-first + random-answer anchor, the 2×2 cell neither paper ran; FAM's anchor decodes 97.91% DEGENERATE | strong |
| **Single-axis evaluation is unsafe here** | three axes give three different winners; both parent papers report ROUGE-L/probability, which a degenerate model aces | strong, reviewable |
| **Eight candidates closed on pre-registered rules** | `FINDING11`–`FINDING16` | strong |
| **ATWU adaptation underperforms** | 11 configurations (ρ, β, lr, steps, selector, loss) | moderate — the MDLM adaptation is ours, not theirs |

## 2. What is NOT true, and I nearly published two of these

**(a) AFC is not more robust — it is materially LESS robust.** Valid attack (positive control
passed: T +0.475), matched starting disclosure (both H = 0.225), 300 steps:

| arm | H recovery | % of gap to θ₀ |
|---|---|---|
| AFC λ=1 | 0.225 → 0.425 | **35.3%** |
| MDU λ=3 | 0.225 → 0.300 | **13.2%** |

**AFC is 2.7× more reversible.** This is a first-class negative about our own method and
belongs in the headline, not the limitations.

Three RTT runs were needed to get here: lr 1e-4 and lr 1e-5 both produced ~0% recovery that
looked like robustness but was an attack destroying the model (DEGENERATE peaking 0.93–0.98).
The missing instrument was **scoring the attack's own training set T** — if the attack cannot
relearn what it is trained on, a flat curve on held-out data means nothing. That check cost
five lines and would have saved two runs.

**(b) The "redirect vs flatten" mechanism for (a) is refuted.** I hypothesised AFC is more
reversible because it *redirects* mass (low entropy) while MDU *flattens* it (high entropy).
Measured answer-position entropy, n=3 each:

| arm | entropy |
|---|---|
| AFC λ=1 | **2.389 ± 0.556** |
| MDU λ=1 | 1.630 ± 0.157 |
| MDU λ=3 | 1.925 ± 0.125 |

AFC has **higher** entropy and is **more** reversible — the opposite of the hypothesis. We have
**no mechanism** for the robustness gap. Record the fact; do not ship the story.

## 3. What is blocked, measured rather than assumed

**RWKU transfer is not available with this protocol.** The recall screen was a success —
`REAL_KNOWLEDGE_TRANSFER_AVAILABLE`, 20/120 subjects at EM ≥ 0.50, correcting our own closed
study's aggregate `INSUFFICIENT_BASE_RECALL` (0.169 aggregate hides 0.67 on FDR, Genghis Khan,
Jimmy Carter, Karl Marx). But the *task shape* is incompatible:

| | median answer words | facts passing ≥3 salient stems | EOS fraction of canvas |
|---|---|---|---|
| TOFU | **27** | 400 / 400 | — |
| RWKU level 1 | **1** | **19 / 345** | 0.984 at gen 64, 0.938 at gen 16 |
| RWKU level 2 | 2 | — | — |
| RWKU neighbor 1/2 | 1 / 2 | — | — |

All four RWKU levels are cloze/short-answer (max 13 words). The six-cell partition, ASCR-12s
and TV-to-pristine all assume sentence-length answers; DISCLOSE / DEGENERATE / REPLACE /
IGNORANT are not separable for a one-word answer. A launched run confirmed it: 19 usable facts
and 93.5% EOS at the answer positions. Both runs were stopped rather than allowed to produce a
number.

Making RWKU "work" would mean redesigning the metric — and a result on a redesigned metric is
not transfer evidence, it is a new experiment with its own validation burden. **Dream-7B needs a
download (not authorized); the synthetic backbone is confounded (`FINDING5`).** So the result is
**single-dataset, single-backbone, and that is structural rather than a matter of more GPU time.**

## 4. Honest read on the goal

The mandate asked for an algorithm that outperforms the strongest existing methods on a clearly
defined forgetting–utility trade-off. Against that:

- **Delivered:** a configuration that outperforms MDU and EMDU on a clearly defined trade-off,
  multi-seed, with the evaluation protocol argued and the baselines qualified.
- **Not delivered:** a *new algorithm* (AFC is a factorial cell of two published methods — every
  term is theirs); *robustness* (AFC is worse); *transfer* (blocked).

This is an **ablation-and-evaluation contribution**, not a new-method contribution. Presenting
it as the latter would not survive review, and the honest framing is already in
`METHOD_AFC.md` §6 and `RESULTS.md` §5.

## 5. Course correction

**Stop:** chasing RWKU (blocked by task shape), and opening new candidates (eight closed; the
last four were closed by their own pre-registered rules).

**Do, in order:**
1. **Promote the RTT negative to the headline.** A trade-off win that is 2.7× more reversible is
   a materially different claim from a trade-off win, and the paper has to say so up front.
2. **Extend AFC λ=1 to n=5 seeds** — cheap, and the central claim rests on n=3 with one metric
   (DEGENERATE) having sd 0.058 on a 0.117 mean.
3. **Match on DEGENERATE as well as DISCLOSE.** Every comparison so far is matched on disclosure
   only; AFC's degeneracy (0.117) and MDU λ=3's (0.181) differ, so a degeneracy-matched
   comparison is a distinct and fairer cut that has not been run.
4. **Probe the robustness gap** with a measurement rather than a story: adapter weight-norm and
   function-space distance from θ₀ for AFC vs MDU at matched disclosure. If AFC's edit is simply
   *smaller*, that explains both results and is testable — but it is a hypothesis, and it gets
   pre-registered before it is run.

Items 2–4 are ~2 GPU-hours total. Item 1 is writing.
