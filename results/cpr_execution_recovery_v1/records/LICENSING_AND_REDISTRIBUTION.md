# Redistribution terms check (performed 2026-10-19 before publishing new assets)

Verified from primary sources (not memory):
- **FLUX.1 [dev]** — official `black-forest-labs/flux` repository `model_licenses/LICENSE-FLUX1-dev` (v1.1.1), fetched and stored as `records/licenses/LICENSE-FLUX1-dev.txt` (sha256 6f7b3d94ddd94380a4dc2d1211ed57883d320dea96f4393b34d4a454041029e1). The Hugging Face copy of the license is gated (HTTP 401 without a token), so the GitHub copy is the one verified. Relevant terms (§2–§3): access/use/Distribute/create Derivatives **for Non-Commercial Purposes only**; Distribution of the Model or Derivatives requires providing the License, the Attribution Notice, and for Derivatives a modification statement, no implication of endorsement; Outputs may be used for any purpose except as prohibited (no training of competing models); §2(e) conditions use/creation of Outputs on Content Filters or on review of Output for unlawful/infringing content before distribution, plus AI disclosure where required.
- **Decision:** publish LoRA adapters (Derivatives) and generated PNGs with `NOTICE_FLUX1_DEV.txt` + the License copy, labelled non-commercial research only. **Base FLUX.1-dev weights and COCO photographs are not published**; no credentials or private responses are published.
- **Content review (§2(e)(i)(B)):** no content filter is implemented in the pipeline. All prompts are fixed, benign object-scene descriptions (see `configs/cases.json`). Output review before distribution: a contact-sheet scan of every published image by the engineering agent (disclosed in the final report; that scan is a model review, not a legal clearance).
- **GEM** (`multimodal-ai-lab/GEM`): MIT (GitHub license API, SPDX MIT). **RT-DETR r50vd (PekingU)** and **DETR resnet50 (facebook)**: Apache-2.0 per the model-card headers fetched from the Hugging Face raw README. Detector weights are not redistributed; only their prediction outputs are published.
- Limits of this check: I am not a lawyer; the FLUX.1 license summary above is from reading its text; no confirmation from Black Forest Labs was sought.
