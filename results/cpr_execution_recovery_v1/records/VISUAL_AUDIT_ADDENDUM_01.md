## Sample (fixed, balanced, independent of eligibility or quality)
For each test object (toilet, bed): 4 of the 20 target cases plus 1 case from each of the 4 allowed classes (couch, potted plant, bus, train).
Selection: ascending SHA256 of the UTF-8 string `CPR-VISUAL-01|<test_object>|<stratum>|<case_id>` (stratum = `target` or the allowed class name as written in the manifest), ties by lexical case ID; take the first 4 (target) or 1 (allowed class). The same cases are used for all six arms and both training seeds. 8 cases × 2 objects = 16 cases → 16 × 6 × 2 = 192 edited images + 16 matched M0 images = 208 images, all already generated for the primary analysis; no additional generation. Cases are never replaced when missing, poor or ambiguous; missing slots are reported. Code: `scripts/visual_audit.py sample`; output `visual_audit/sample.json`.

## Masking package (offline, static)
- Display copies are lossless PNGs re-encoded from decoded pixels with no metadata and neutral IDs (`A-####` for Pass A, `B-###`/`B-###_ref` for Pass B) in a fixed randomized order. Decoded-pixel identity between every original and display copy is verified and reported; originals are preserved.
- The mapping (neutral ID → arm/seed/case/role/original path) is kept outside the package. A salted commitment `SHA256(salt || canonical_mapping_json)` is committed and pushed to the public repo before any judgment; the salt and mapping stay private until both passes are locked, then are published and verified against the commitment.
- The reviewer receives only the anonymous package, the exact prompt text and the named object per image, and this rubric: no method names, detector scores, training results, seeds or mapping.
- Reviewer: a fresh, isolated model reviewer context (Claude subagent with existing image-viewing capability, instructed to read only the package directory) that did not see this study's history. Judgments are labelled `MODEL_ASSISTED_VISUAL_SCREEN`; they are not human validation or ground truth. Limitations: the subagent shares a filesystem with the engineering agent (it is instructed not to look outside the package and its transcript is kept), reviews may be chunked across several fresh contexts to fit context limits (chunk assignment is a fixed slice of the randomized order and is disclosed), and rater calibration may drift across chunks. If clean-context review cannot be run, the inspection is labelled unblinded and the package preserved.

## Pass A — individual-image assessment (all 208 anonymous images incl. masked M0)
Per image the reviewer sees the image, the exact prompt and the named object (no method or role labels) and records:
- Object presence of the named object: `clearly_present` / `recognizable_part_present` (clearly identifiable partial objects count as presence) / `absent` / `uncertain` (ambiguous malformed shapes are uncertain) / `unjudgeable`.
- Absolute rendering: `coherent` / `coherent_localized_defects` / `substantial_distortions_interpretable` / `pervasive_degeneration` / `uncertain_unjudgeable`.
- A brief reason for every concerning case (any rendering other than `coherent`, or `uncertain`/`unjudgeable`).
Rules: painting-like style alone is not degeneration; target absence or a different coherent scene is not automatically a rendering failure; pervasive corruption cannot establish successful erasure.
Pass A is saved and hashed (`PASS_A_JUDGMENTS.json`, SHA256 committed) before pairings are revealed.

## Pass B — paired relative-rendering assessment (192 edited vs labeled M0 reference)
Each anonymous edited image is shown beside its labeled M0 reference (matched prompt and noise) with the prompt. Methods, seeds and detector outcomes stay hidden. The reviewer judges relative rendering: `better` / `similar` / `worse` / `uncertain`, and damage extent: `none` / `localized` / `pervasive` / `uncertain`. M0 is not assumed flawless; pixel identity is not required; target absence itself is not penalized. Pass B is locked and hashed (`PASS_B_JUDGMENTS.json`) before unblinding or any comparison with detectors. Both passes are preserved without overwriting.

## Analysis (after unblinding; descriptive; no change to the experiment)
Targets: conditional removal = M0 presence (clearly/recognizable) AND edited absence; also removal with acceptable coherence (coherent or localized defects only); also the stricter subset judged no worse than M0. Allowed classes: recognizable presence and quality preservation, M0-positive conditional and all-sampled counts. Denominators are always shown; uncertain, unjudgeable and missing are never converted to success. Detector nondetections are compared with visual presence/partial/uncertain and with substantial damage. The frozen detector analysis and decision rules are unchanged. A separate visual-evidence conclusion is reported: `corroborating` / `concerning` / `insufficient`. A detector advantage with rendering damage is not established semantic or quality superiority.
Data-integrity: genuine file/association errors are disclosed and the erroneous records preserved.
Limits: four distinct target cases per object; methods and seed repetitions are not additional independent prompts; no powered semantic validation or general efficacy claim; allowed-only images do not establish background preservation within the same erased scene. No tuning, regeneration, case expansion or objective revision from judgments. If a semantic conclusion hinges on unresolved ambiguity, the smallest exact set needing independent human review is listed and the claim is marked pending (no reviewers contacted; numerical publication is not delayed).

## Cost accounting
No GPU, training, generation or download is used by the audit. Packaging (CPU) and review (model tokens) are charged to the ledger as CPU jobs or noted as unmetered model-review cost.
