### 5.2 Lower bounds including unclosed jobs

**Method.** Each ledger line carries a snapshot that is cumulative *within its
own process*. For every distinct PID take the **maximum** such snapshot it ever
wrote, then sum across PIDs. One maximum per PID means no process is counted
twice, and summing distinct PIDs means none is missed.

| Quantity | Lower bound |
|---|---|
| GPU device-seconds | **545.611** = **0.1515586 h** |
| CPU core-seconds | **594.584** = **0.1651622 h** |

45.181 / 19.114 / 18.657 / 14.055 / 13.137 / 0.168 · release_verification
13.645 · scorer_qualification 5.776 · cpu_entry_gate 0.000 · entry_gate_stop
0.000.

**These are lower bounds, not exact totals.** A killed process stops writing,
so cost incurred after its last line is invisible. Full costs remain unknown
where unrecorded. **Nothing was rerun to estimate them.**
