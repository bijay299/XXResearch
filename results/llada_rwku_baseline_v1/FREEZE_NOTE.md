## What is frozen

- **Candidate pool:** the first ten canonical RWKU target entities (`1_Stephen_King` … `10_Prince_Harry,_Duke_of_Sussex`).
- **Joint dedup across forget levels 1 and 2** keyed on normalized answer; deterministic order (level-2 simple questions before level-1 cloze). All ten entities yielded ≥11 distinct unaided facts, so **zero source-insufficient entities** — recorded before inference.
- **Ten distinct qualification facts per entity**, drawn only from unaided-recall items.
- **Answer-supplying attacks separated:** any item whose query already contains its own normalized answer is excluded from qualification and preserved separately. These test copying, not unaided recall.
- **References + conservative aliases**, generated by frozen rule (self, parenthetical-stripped, `&`/`and`, whole-reference small-integer digit↔word, whole-reference US variants). No partial/substring forms; no surname-only for a full personal name.
- **Answer-only instructions** (separate templates for question and cloze items) and the MMLU single-letter template, verbatim.
- **Normalization, parsers and metrics.** Primary = normalized short-answer **exact match** (full-string equality after normalization). Substring containment is *not* called exact match. Token-boundary span recall and ROUGE-L recall are reported separately, as are empty / refusal / repetition / truncation / parse failures.
- **Shared 40-question MMLU utility panel**, selected deterministically (seed 20261020).
- **Neighbor conflict labelling**, by two independent pre-inference signals:
  1. *name* — target name tokens appear in the item's query/answer;
  2. *passage overlap* — the item's topic tokens **and** its full answer co-occur **within a single chunk** of the target's own training passages, i.e. the fact is stated in the material we will train on.

  Signal (2) catches semantic conflicts that never name the target: e.g. all three
  *"Spare is a memoir released on ___ January 2023"* items for Prince Harry are
  flagged `name=False, passage=True` — *Spare* is his own memoir.
  **Raw benchmark metrics over all neighbor items are preserved**; nonconflicting
  locality is reported as a separate, labelled quantity.
  *Limitation:* both signals are lexical. A semantic conflict whose topic title and
  answer tokens are absent from the passage corpus can still slip through.

## Source-count resolution, before committing panel counts

- Qualification facts: all 10 entities supply the required 10. No shortfall.
- Nonconflicting neighbors: 9 of 10 entities supply ≥10. **`10_Prince_Harry,_Duke_of_Sussex` supplies only 7** (53 of 60 neighbor items conflict). That shortfall is recorded in `frozen/frozen_neighbor_availability.json`; if the entity is selected its neighbor panel stays at 7 and is **never** padded with duplicate or conflicting items.

## Honest framing

The baseline to be built is an **MDU-objective LoRA adaptation on LLaDA/RWKU**. It is
neither our own new method nor an exact reproduction of MDU's full-parameter published
results. All material produced here is **engineering/development data**, not untouched
final evidence for any future candidate method.

`open-dlu` is consulted for implementation detail only. Its RWKU loader is
**deliberately not adopted**: `load_rwku_forget` appends `f"{query} {answer}"` from
`forget_level1`/`forget_level2` into the training corpus, i.e. it trains on the very
evaluation questions, which would contaminate any forgetting measurement.
